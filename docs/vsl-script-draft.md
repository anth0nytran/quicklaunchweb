# VSL draft: "Watch this before our call" (~6–7 min)

For the top of `/call-booked` and the booking-confirmation text. Rewrite it in your own words.
Film it on a real job site, not at a desk. That's the whole pitch.
When it's on YouTube, paste the video ID into `lib/vsl.ts`.

Hormozi structure: promise → pain → proof → old way vs. our way → objections → proof → CTA that proves they watched.
Simple words. No client lead counts or ad spend (your rule). Only stats that have a source.

---

## 1. Promise (0:00–0:30)
"Hey, it's Anthony with QuickLaunchWeb. You just booked a call with me. Thank you.
In the next few minutes I'll show you exactly how we get Houston contractors more calls, more booked jobs, and more money in their pocket.
Watch this whole thing. Our call will be twice as useful, because we can skip the basics and get right to *your* numbers."

## 2. The pain (0:30–1:30)
- "You're great at the work. But the phone isn't steady."
- "Maybe you're paying Angi or HomeAdvisor, and the same lead goes to four other guys. Now it's a price war."
- "Maybe you tried boosting a post. You got likes, not jobs."
- "Maybe leads do come in, but you're on a roof and can't call back. By the time you do, they hired somebody else."
- "None of that is a you-are-bad-at-business problem. It's a system problem."

## 3. Proof, local (1:30–2:15)
- Show the client logo wall. Name trades and areas: "fencing in ___, insulation in ___, roofing in ___."
- Read one real client quote (only approved ones).
- Use a sourced stat: "85% of people say a video has convinced them to buy. That's from Wyzowl's 2026 survey. Homeowners want to see who's coming to their house."

## 4. Old way vs. our way (2:15–3:30)
| Old way | Our way |
|---|---|
| Stock photos or boosted posts | We film *you* on real Houston jobs |
| Ads shown to all of Texas | Ads aimed at homeowners in the parts of town you want, for the jobs you want |
| Lead sits for hours | Every lead gets a text in seconds |
| You chase leads between jobs | Our team calls, checks they're a real homeowner with a real project, and books the estimate |
| Agency reports likes and clicks | We look at booked jobs and revenue |

"And we only take one business per trade per area. If we work with you, we don't work with the guy down the street."

## 5. Objections (3:30–5:30)
Use the same pattern for each one: what people think, why it's wrong, what's true, then proof.

1. **"Facebook ads don't work for contractors."**
   Most people who say that boosted a post. Real lead campaigns with real video, plus fast follow-up, are a different thing.
2. **"Facebook leads are junk."**
   Some are, if the form doesn't filter and nobody calls fast. Our forms ask the right questions, and our team qualifies every lead before it reaches your calendar.
3. **"I don't want to be on camera."**
   About one hour of your time. No script. We can film your crew and your work, or run photo ads.
4. **"I'll lose my ad account if I leave."**
   It's in your name, on your card. You keep everything.
5. **"How much is it?"**
   There are two numbers: our fee, and the ad money that goes straight to Meta. We'll go through both on the call, against what your average job is worth.
6. **"I'm too busy."**
   That's the point. We film it, run it, follow up, and book it.

## 6. Proof again (5:30–6:15)
Use a short testimonial clip or before/after ad footage from a client who gave permission.

## 7. CTA that proves they watched (6:15–6:45)
"Here's what I want you to do. Text the word **WATCHED** to 832-552-3458.
Then before our call, have two numbers ready: what your average job is worth, and how many more jobs a month you could take on.
See you on the call."
